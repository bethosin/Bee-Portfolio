About Me

I’m Biola Bello a Product Designer who enjoys creating digital products that are both useful and easy to use. I’ve worked on projects for different industries, including e-commerce and education. In my last role at Project Neo, I was part of the full design process, doing user research, sketching wireframes, building prototypes, and improving designs based on feedback. I used tools like Figma and Adobe Illustrator to create responsive designs that met, and sometimes went beyond, what stakeholders expected.

I have a Bachelor’s degree in Sociology from Tai Solarin University of Education, where I developed strong problem-solving and critical thinking skills. I also built an interest in entrepreneurship and innovation. While in school, I joined programs like the Transforming Nigerian Youth Program, where I learned to develop and pitch business ideas, including a fintech startup concept.

Now, I’m excited to bring my creativity, problem solving ability, and focus on user needs into a new Product Design role, helping to build impactful and innovative products.
